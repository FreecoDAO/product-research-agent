# Freeco AI Organizational Chart

## Executive Board
*   **CEO (AI Orchestrator):** Oversees overall company strategy, cross-department coordination, and mission alignment (Sustainability & High-End Concierge).
    *   *Proposed Implementation:* **Manus Agent via MCP** (providing deep contextual reasoning and sandboxed execution) or **Hermes Agent** (providing autonomous self-improving skills and persistent memory). Both offer the holistic, multi-step logic required for the CEO role.
*   **CFO (AI Agent):** Manages Paperclip-Surfers budget, API token limits, financial forecasting, and invoice approvals.
*   **Head of Operations (Concierge Service):** Coordinates multi-agent workflows, ensures UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol) compliance, and manages service delivery logistics.
    *   *Proposed Implementation:* **OpenFang Orchestrator Agent** (Tier 1 - DeepSeek), utilizing its robust task decomposition and delegation capabilities.
*   **Head of Business Development:** Identifies new market opportunities, partnership strategies (e.g., sustainable brands), and growth vectors.
*   **Head of Marketing:** Manages brand positioning (Swiss high-end), content generation, and customer engagement strategies.
*   **Head of Sales:** Drives user acquisition, subscription conversions, and high-value concierge service upsells.

## Shopping Department (The Concierge Team)
*   **Head of Shopping:** Orchestrates the product research and recommendation pipeline.
    *   **Freeco Shopping Concierge (Primary Agent):**
        *   **Role:** Direct user interaction, intent parsing, and final recommendation delivery.
        *   **Capabilities:** Enforces the "100% Organic & Vegan" strict policy. Formats output into the signature 3-Tier Markdown Table (Best Price, Best Value, Luxury).
        *   **Tools:** Tavily Search API, Novita AI (DeepSeek V4 Flash).
    *   **Product Researcher (Sub-Agent):** Conducts deep-dive LangGraph research across multiple sources to find luxury and niche items.
    *   **Review Analyzer (Sub-Agent):** Scrapes and analyzes customer reviews to ensure the "Best Choice" tier is genuinely highly rated and reliable.

## Data & Infrastructure
*   **Marketplace Manager:** Maintains the WooCommerce/Dokan backend and ensures synchronization with the Open Food Facts database.
*   **Security & Compliance Officer:** Monitors agent logs (via Paperclip) to ensure data privacy (Swiss standards) and adherence to ethical AI guidelines.
